# Image assets

All production images are local WebP files under `public/images/`. They are illustrative, not company employees, endorsements, offices or evidence of destination partnerships.

## Original generated hero (retained earlier variant)

`public/images/hero-workers.webp` was created with the built-in image generation tool, using the **imagegen** skill. The generated source remains in the tool’s original generated-images location. The project uses an optimized 1942 × 809 WebP copy.

Final generation prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: cinematic hero photograph for a premium Indian overseas manpower recruitment website. Create a very wide landscape editorial photograph, approximately 2.4:1 aspect ratio. Three Indian skilled industrial professionals standing together confidently at a modern construction and manufacturing site: foreground Indian male engineer in white hardhat, navy workwear and clean orange safety vest, a female Indian engineer in white hardhat and navy safety workwear, and another Indian male technician in yellow hardhat and navy workwear. They look dignified, hopeful and natural, not overly staged. Frame the workers on the right half, visible waist up, with the main male engineer near 74% across the image. Keep the entire left 45% as very dark navy industrial architecture and soft distant construction detail, with no people, suitable for white headline overlay. Modern steel construction structures and cranes softly out of focus behind the people. Warm late-afternoon sidelight on authentic Indian faces, subtle cinematic grain, navy and warm grey palette with muted amber accents. Documentary-quality, natural skin textures, professional safety equipment, realistic anatomy and hands. No text, watermarks, logos, flags, or company branding. No visual implication of real company endorsement.

## Editorial refinement: current imagery

The current homepage hero is **`public/images/hero-workshop.webp`** (1448 × 1086, approximately 134 KB). The human story and About-page hero use **`public/images/worker-portrait.webp`** (1536 × 1024, approximately 135 KB). Both were generated with the built-in image generation tool and the imagegen skill, then compressed into project-local WebP assets. Their original generated files are preserved. These fictional people illustrate workforce skills; they do not represent company staff or endorsements.

Final hero prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: premium international workforce recruitment website photography, a real-feeling editorial image, not a website mockup. A candid medium-wide photograph of two Indian skilled manufacturing professionals collaborating at a precision engineering workbench inside a clean, working metal fabrication factory. Indian woman in her early thirties in navy work shirt, white safety hardhat and clear safety glasses, holding a simple unbranded clipboard and attentively looking at a metal component; Indian male technician in his forties in faded navy coveralls, yellow hardhat and clear safety glasses carefully inspecting the component with a caliper. Both appear highly competent and dignified, natural expressions, not posing for the camera. Hands realistic and anatomically accurate. Machine tools, steel rafters, practical workshop details in soft focus behind them. Diffuse daylight from tall factory windows, restrained warm neutral light, filmic texture, navy, muted brass, grey and warm white palette. Frame both people close to the middle of the image so it supports both a portrait crop for desktop and a horizontal crop for mobile. Faces and working hands must remain clearly visible in the central area. A 4:3 landscape image. No text, branding, logos, watermarks, artificially golden sunset, dramatic sparks, or stock-business posing. Illustrative fictional people, no actual company endorsement.

Final portrait prompt:

> Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: human-centered editorial workforce photograph for a premium Indian international recruitment website. A dignified Indian skilled tradesman in his late thirties standing at a real industrial workshop bench, looking thoughtfully slightly past the camera with a very subtle natural smile. He wears a naturally worn navy work shirt with sleeves rolled to the wrists and plain personal protective equipment appropriate to a workshop, no hardhat indoors. Calloused working hands resting on the bench next to a simple precision metal component. Background has tools and machinery with rich natural material textures, light from tall windows. The person feels specific and human, no model-like corporate glamour, no artificial skin smoothness. Chest-up to waist-up composition, eyes near upper third, the man positioned slightly to the left of center, workshop space surrounding him. Restrained cinematic documentary photography, a 3:2 landscape image, natural soft side lighting, subdued navy and warm grey palette, modest warm highlights. Confident, respectful, human, hopeful. No dramatic sparks, artificial sun flare, text, logo, watermark or implication that this fictional person endorses a real company.
